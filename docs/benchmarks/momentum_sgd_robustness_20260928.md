# K3 Momentum SGD robustness under noise, preferred views, and shifts

The fixed coarse-grid Momentum SGD comparison has **condition-dependent recovery**, not general three-state robustness. At half the original noise variance, it recovers GT states 0 and 1 but gives up substantial state-2 quality and recall relative to native VDAM. With preferred orientations, it recovers all three states more clearly than the matched native control in both seeds, though GT0 remains the weakest map. At twice the noise variance and with nonzero particle shifts, both arms have weak maps and approximately random all-particle pose medians. Neither condition supports a successful joint pose/class/shift claim. The [standing scorecard](../math/momentum_sgd_robustness_scorecard.md) shows every seed and state; its [JSON](../math/momentum_sgd_robustness_scorecard.json) retains the conditional pose, recall, shift, occupancy, class-pair FSC and timing values.

## Fixed comparison

Each condition has 20,000 balanced signed GT particles, box 64, 6 Å pixels, the same three GT volumes, CTFs, labels and particle order. The training STAR has neutral poses and origins; GT appears only in evaluation. The baseline noise-1 fixture has measured 128-image probe SNR 0.032209. The new fixtures change one distribution at a time: noise variance ×0.5 (SNR 0.064417), noise variance ×2 (SNR 0.016104), an 80% preferred-orientation mixture within antipodal 30° cones plus 20% uniform directions (SNR 0.033148), or isotropic particle shifts with 2 px component standard deviation truncated at 4 px radius (SNR 0.032209). The preferred mixture retains full angular support, but directions outside the cones are weakly sampled. The independent fixture audit (`/scratch/gpfs/CRYOEM/gilleslab/em_work/cryosparc_sgd_robustness_20260928/analysis_conditions/condition_audit.json`; outside the repository) verified all hashes, 20,000 neutral training rows, all 128 clean projection probes per condition and shared noise-residual construction. Its empirical preferred-cone fraction is 82.675%; shifted-particle RMS radius is 2.354 px.

The frozen scientific source was `ae1165193c3d75afa64ffb1d6524e85b75237538`; native libraries have source fingerprint `211d2f14684542eaf652f1a107875f2eb1cd204735de09678f620f01496e3032`. Historical run records use arm ID `cryosparc_sgd`; the current public name is Momentum SGD. The later naming branch did not produce these results. Both arms ran production float32 with K3, fixed HEALPix order 3, oversampling 0, batch 300 for all 600 updates, radii 15×100 then 24×200 then 31×300, uniform class/direction priors, positivity off, and the same significant coarse-support cap of 300. The Momentum SGD learning rate was 0.4, as in the prior noise-1 run. Both arms retain the shared [mixed-unit RELION translation prior](https://github.com/ma-gilles/relax/issues/6#issuecomment-5875803217); no physical-prior correction was applied.

Initial maps and training STARs matched within each condition/seed pair. Actual particle and halfset ID hashes matched at all twelve saved 50-update checkpoints for each seed; intervening per-update hashes were not retained. Each pair used two A100-SXM4-80GB GPUs in one job, with arm placement swapped for seed 12. Bootstrap maps can differ *between* conditions because initialization reads the changed images. The full frozen scorecard (`/scratch/gpfs/CRYOEM/gilleslab/em_work/cryosparc_sgd_robustness_20260928/analysis_conditions/combined_scorecard.json`; outside the repository) records source, image and map hashes and GPU UUIDs.

## What the results establish

Maps were independently rigid/hand-registered to signed GT. Reported FSC is the unmasked mean over shells 1–15. The table gives GT states **0 / 1 / 2**. Conditional pose and shift statistics use only particles assigned to the matched GT-state class and must be read with recall and sample count in the scorecard. STAR poses, classes and origins are each particle's **last visited minibatch** estimate, not fresh inference on the final maps.

In the half-noise condition, Momentum SGD GT2 assignment recall falls to .379/.117 in seeds 11/12, versus .999/.998 for native VDAM. In the preferred-view condition, Momentum SGD GT0 recall is .965/.949, but its GT0 FSC is .554/.665, lower than its other states. High-noise and shifted fits have all-particle pose medians near 130° in both arms despite some relative FSC improvements. For shifted particles, all-assigned calibrated median shift errors are 4.341/4.235 px for native and 3.156/3.479 px for Momentum SGD; these are not evidence of good joint recovery when classes and poses are wrong. The correct-class, pose <10° shifted subsets contain only 3–6 particles per arm. The independent native/simulator shift-boundary proof (`/scratch/gpfs/CRYOEM/gilleslab/em_work/cryosparc_sgd_robustness_20260928/protocol/shift_boundary.json`; outside the repository) had maximum fitted-origin residual 0.00177 px, qualifying the measurement convention rather than the optimizer's accuracy.

All 16 arms completed 600 updates with finite maps and noise spectra; no exact duplicate class maps were found. Those operational checks do not establish three-state recovery. Reported pass-2 class fractions are normalized **after** coarse significant-support selection, not global unpruned occupancy; model STAR fractions are the fixed uniform prior. The inherited cap may matter scientifically, but this matrix does not isolate its effect. The registered map panels (`/scratch/gpfs/CRYOEM/gilleslab/em_work/cryosparc_sgd_robustness_20260928/analysis_conditions/registered_panels.md`; outside the repository) and FSC curves (`/scratch/gpfs/CRYOEM/gilleslab/em_work/cryosparc_sgd_robustness_20260928/analysis_conditions/combined_report.md`; outside the repository) retain visual and shell-level evidence. The separate density-energy centroid diagnostic (`/scratch/gpfs/CRYOEM/gilleslab/em_work/cryosparc_sgd_robustness_20260928/analysis_conditions/centering_diagnostic.md`; outside the repository) measures raw-frame `Σ(index·V²)/ΣV²`, **not** physical center of mass. The maximum candidate norm at iteration 600 is 6.735 voxels, while the three unregistered GT norms are 9.199, 4.895 and 1.625 voxels. There was no imposed origin constraint, and the centroid alone is no quality gate or causal explanation.

## Execution and cost

GPU jobs `14633155`–`14633158` and CPU evaluation jobs `14633307`–`14633310` all completed `0:0`; fixture generation job `14633054` also completed `0:0`. The four paired two-A100 allocations ran 1574, 1578, 1564 and 1559 seconds, or **3.486111 allocated GPU-hours** total. The 600-update arm wall times are 773–786 seconds; Momentum/native ratios range .993–1.006. Across all arms, whole-update medians are .865–.884 s at radius 15, 1.079–1.110 s at radius 24, and 1.344–1.368 s at radius 31 (phase timing (`/scratch/gpfs/CRYOEM/gilleslab/em_work/cryosparc_sgd_robustness_20260928/analysis_conditions/update_timing.md`; outside the repository)). CPU evaluations took 589, 587, 584 and 594 seconds, and fixture generation took 154 seconds. Arm wall times include startup, updates and checkpoint output, with matched hardware per pair; there is no material measured optimizer speed difference here. The saved run roots (`/scratch/gpfs/CRYOEM/gilleslab/em_work/cryosparc_sgd_robustness_20260928/runs`; outside the repository) and protocol (`/scratch/gpfs/CRYOEM/gilleslab/em_work/cryosparc_sgd_robustness_20260928/protocol`; outside the repository) preserve exact inputs, invocations and outputs.

The earlier uniform noise-1 HP3 r3 experiment used source `f81e815e33b160511526a580e48905bb3c8ae229`, while this matrix used `ae116519`; changed inputs and source make cross-suite differences contextual, not a matched causal contrast. Two seeds per condition and no per-condition retuning cannot estimate a general success probability. These results show condition-dependent, partial recovery: the preferred-view candidate recovers GT1/2 strongly and GT0 more weakly, while other conditions have substantial state or pose failures. They do not establish generally robust three-state recovery. No acceptance threshold was fitted to the observed results.
